The Legion

The Arcis ambassador corps. Its purpose is not reach — it is durable ownership: real holders, real integrations, real builders who stay. One law governs it: reward ownership and outcomes, never activity. We don't pay per post. We reward proven impact.

The ranks

CitizenEntered the Citadel — a badge, community access, and a path to climb.
LegionaryActive contributor — revenue-reward eligibility, private channel, early access.
CenturionLeads a cohort — higher share, direct founder line, roadmap input.
PraetorianElite, by nomination — top share, governance weight, represents Arcis.
ConsulInner circle — governance authority and a voice in protocol direction.

How it works

  • Rank is earned, not bought. Ship integrations, onboard depositors, create real content, lead the community — that's what moves you up.
  • Rewards come from real revenue. CUSTOS earns USDC; the Legion is paid from that, retroactively each quarter — not from token emissions.
  • No farming. On-chain proof of holding and contribution, reviewed by hand. Status, access, and a share of what the protocol actually earns.
